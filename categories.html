Explore Food Categories

Discover nutritious foods and learn about their health benefits.

🍎 Fruits

Examples: Apples, bananas, oranges, mangoes.

Nutrients: Vitamins, minerals and fiber.

Benefits: Support digestion and overall health.

🥦 Vegetables

Examples: Carrots, spinach, broccoli, tomatoes.

Nutrients: Vitamins, minerals and fiber.

Benefits: Help maintain a balanced diet.

🌾 Whole Grains

Examples: Oats, brown rice, wheat and millets.

Nutrients: Carbohydrates and fiber.

Benefits: Provide energy for daily activities.

🥜 Nuts and Seeds

Examples: Almonds, peanuts, walnuts and chia seeds.

Nutrients: Healthy fats, protein and minerals.

Benefits: Support a nutritious eating pattern.

🥛 Dairy Foods

Examples: Milk, curd, yogurt and paneer.

Nutrients: Protein and calcium.

Benefits: Help maintain strong bones and muscles.

🫘 Protein Foods

Examples: Dal, chickpeas, beans, eggs and tofu.

Nutrients: Protein, iron and other minerals.

Benefits: Help build and repair body tissues.

Eat a Variety of Healthy Foods!

Choose different fruits, vegetables, whole grains and protein-rich foods to enjoy a balanced diet.
