Healthy and Delicious Recipes

Discover easy recipes made with fresh and nutritious ingredients.

🥗 Vegetable Salad

A refreshing salad full of colorful vegetables.

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Calories: Approximately 150 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 tomato
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 carrot
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Preparation

  1. Wash all the vegetables.
  2. Cut them into small pieces.
  3. Mix the vegetables in a bowl.
  4. Add lemon juice, salt and pepper.
  5. Mix well and serve fresh.

🥣 Fruit Oatmeal

A nutritious breakfast to start your day.

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Calories: Approximately 300 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • Half a cup of oats
  • 1 cup of milk
  • Half a banana
  • Apple slices
  • A few almonds

Preparation

  1. Add oats and milk to a pan.
  2. Cook until the oats become soft.
  3. Slice the banana and apple.
  4. Add the fruits and almonds.
  5. Serve warm.

🍲 Vegetable Dal

A comforting lentil dish rich in plant protein.

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Calories: Approximately 220 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • Half a cup of lentils
  • 1 tomato
  • Half an onion
  • Turmeric powder
  • Salt and spices
  • Water as needed

Preparation

  1. Wash the lentils thoroughly.
  2. Cook lentils with water until soft.
  3. Add chopped tomato and onion.
  4. Add turmeric, salt and spices.
  5. Cook until everything is tender.

🥪 Vegetable Sandwich

A quick and tasty snack with fresh vegetables.

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Calories: Approximately 250 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 2 slices whole-wheat bread
  • Cucumber slices
  • Tomato slices
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Curd spread or hummus

Preparation

  1. Wash and slice the vegetables.
  2. Spread curd or hummus on the bread.
  3. Add cucumber, tomato and lettuce.
  4. Cover with the second bread slice.
  5. Cut and serve fresh.

🥞 Healthy Vegetable Dosa

A delicious Indian breakfast with vegetables.

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Calories: Approximately 200 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 cup dosa batter
  • Chopped onion
  • Grated carrot
  • Chopped coriander
  • A little cooking oil

Preparation

  1. Heat a non-stick pan.
  2. Spread the dosa batter evenly.
  3. Add chopped vegetables on top.
  4. Cook with a little oil until crisp.
  5. Serve with sambar or chutney.

🍌 Banana Smoothie

A simple fruit drink made with milk and banana.

Preparation Time: 5 minutes

Calories: Approximately 200 kcal per serving

Ingredients

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1 cup of milk
  • 2 almonds
  • A pinch of cinnamon (optional)

Preparation

  1. Peel and slice the banana.
  2. Add banana and milk to a blender.
  3. Blend until smooth.
  4. Add almonds and cinnamon if desired.
  5. Pour into a glass and serve.
